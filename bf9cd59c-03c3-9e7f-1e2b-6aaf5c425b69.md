Meshes are generated during triangulation of faces. They can also be encountered directly in Revit geometry (typically imported geometry). Meshes contain a single array of [Vertices](32b34134-9301-f952-a910-5a34d0d0235d.md), and a corresponding array of triangles. Triangles can be accessed by index from [TriangleInt32](35abd642-cf15-8281-e45d-083fb5c53d2a.md), and reference 3 vertices from the [Vertices](32b34134-9301-f952-a910-5a34d0d0235d.md) array.

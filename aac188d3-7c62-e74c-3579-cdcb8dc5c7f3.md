#### Return Value

ISet[ElementId](44f3f7b1-3229-3404-93c9-dc5e70337dd6.md) The ids of levels the railing is placed on. The returned set consists of a subset of the base level ids of the corresponding stairs in the [MultistoryStairs](8b07cbff-013c-889f-8807-703e63a91923.md).

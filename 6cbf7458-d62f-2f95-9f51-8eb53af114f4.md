#### Parameters

layerIdx Int32
     Index of a layer in the CompoundStructure. The layer index is zero based. It counts from the exterior of wall and from the top of roofs, floors and ceilings. 
priority Int32
     The priority of the layer to be checked.

Boolean

Membrane layers should not have priority, only 999 is allowed. For layers of other functions, priority value should be between 1 and 5.
